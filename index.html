About us

A second home for your little one

Tucked in the heart of Maringphai, Grace Baby Class is a small, caring preschool where children take their very first steps into the world of learning. We believe childhood is not a race — it is a journey to be savoured with paint-stained hands, giggles in the sand and stories under a warm blanket.

Our gentle, play-based approach helps every child grow in confidence, curiosity and kindness — at their own pace, in their own way.

  • Warm, trained and patient teachers
  • Safe, clean and child-friendly classrooms
  • Small groups so no child is left behind
  • Rhymes, stories, art, movement & outdoor play

Questions?

Little answers for big questions

What is the right age to join?

Children can join Playgroup at 2 years. Nursery, LKG and UKG follow at ages 3, 4 and 5 respectively.

What are the school timings?

Monday to Saturday, from 9:00 AM. Pick-up is in the early afternoon — exact timings are shared during admission.

What should my child bring?

Just a small snack box, a water bottle and a spare set of clothes. We provide all learning and art materials.

How do you help first-time schoolers settle?

With a gentle settling-in period: short initial days, one-on-one teacher time, and lots of reassurance for both child and parents.
