About us

A second home for your little one

Tucked in the heart of Maringphai, Grace Baby School is a small, caring school where children take their very first steps into the world of learning. We believe childhood is not a race — it is a journey to be savoured with paint-stained hands, giggles in the sand and stories under a warm blanket.

Our gentle, play-based approach helps every child grow in confidence, curiosity and kindness — at their own pace, in their own way.

  • Warm, trained and patient teachers
  • Safe, clean and child-friendly classrooms
  • Small groups so no child is left behind
  • One school family from Playgroup to Class III
  • Rhymes, stories, art, movement & outdoor play

Our programs

Little steps, right on time

Every class, from Playgroup to Class III, is designed around how children grow — never hurried, always encouraged.

🐣

Playgroup

2 – 3 years

A soft landing into school life with sensory play, rhymes, and lots of cuddles as little ones learn to be away from home.

🌱

Nursery

3 – 4 years

Colours, shapes, sounds and songs — building vocabulary, motor skills and the joy of "I did it myself!"

✏️

LKG

4 – 5 years

Pre-writing strokes, phonics foundations, numbers through play and plenty of storytelling and craft.

UKG

5 – 6 years

Class I

A confident start to primary school — reading fluency, numbers to 100, EVS and show-and-tell expression.

Class II

7 – 8 years

Writing sentences with growing confidence, addition & subtraction, and hands-on project discovery.

Class III

8 – 9 years

Strong primary foundations — grammar & short paragraphs, multiplication & division, and enquiry-based science.

Questions?

Little answers for big questions

What is the right age to join?

Children can join Playgroup at 2 years and grow with us right through Nursery, LKG and UKG to Class I–III — up to age 9 — in one familiar, loving school family.

What are the school timings?

Monday to Saturday, from 9:00 AM. Pick-up is in the early afternoon — exact timings are shared during admission.

What should my child bring?

Just a small snack box, a water bottle and a spare set of clothes. We provide all learning and art materials.

How do you help first-time schoolers settle?

With a gentle settling-in period: short initial days, one-on-one teacher time, and lots of reassurance for both child and parents.
